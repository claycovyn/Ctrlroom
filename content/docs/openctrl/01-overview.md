# OpenCtrl

OpenCtrl is a collective that builds apps for its community. These docs cover every OpenCtrl app in one place.

## Apps

- **Ctrl Room**: community chat with rooms, threads, voice and video. Start at [Getting started](/docs/ctrl-room/getting-started).

More apps will be added here as they ship.

## Getting help

- In any Ctrl Room room, type **@openctrl** and your question. It answers from these docs, in a thread under your message.
- Type **@ctrl** for anything else: general questions, explanations or research on the web.
- Can't find an answer? Ask in your community's #general room.
