# Getting started

## Create an account

Go to the Ctrl Room home page and choose **Get started**. You need:

- A **display name**: what people see next to your messages.
- A **username**: 2–32 letters, numbers or underscores. People @mention you with it.
- An **email** and a **password** of at least 8 characters.

If email confirmation is turned on, Ctrl Room sends you a link to confirm your account before you can sign in.

One account works for every community you join.

## Start a community

After signing in you land on **Your communities**. Under **Start a community**, fill in:

- **Icon**: an emoji or a short label (optional).
- **Name**: shown in the sidebar.
- **Link**: the address of your community, `/c/your-link`. It's made from the name, and you can edit it.
- **What's it for?**: a one-line description (optional).

Every new community starts with three rooms: **#announcements** (only mods can start posts), **#general**, and a voice room called **lounge**. You're the **owner**.

## Join a community

Paste an invite link or code under **Join with an invite**, or just open the invite link. If you aren't signed in yet, Ctrl Room asks you to create an account or sign in first, then adds you to the community.

## Find your way around

- The **left rail** lists your communities. The **+** button takes you back to create or join another.
- The **sidebar** lists the community's rooms, with voice rooms in their own group.
- The **header** of each room has buttons for calls, the room board, search, people and notifications.
- The **gear** next to the community name opens community settings.
