# Direct messages

Direct messages are conversations outside any room: one other person, or a group of up to 10 people. Open them with the **messages** button at the top of the community bar, next to your communities. The number on it counts unread messages and new requests.

Only the people in a conversation can read it. Direct messages don't belong to any community, so they don't count toward a community's OpenCtrl Cloud usage.

## Start a conversation

1. Open direct messages and choose **New message**.
2. Search for people you share a community with. Pick one person, or up to nine for a group (you can name the group).
3. Write the first message and send it.

Messaging the same person again always opens your existing conversation with them.

## Requests

The first message from someone you haven't talked with lands in **Requests**. You can read it, but you can't reply until you **Accept**. If you **Ignore** it, it moves to the Ignored list and won't notify you again. You can still accept it later.

## Who can message you

In **Settings → Privacy**, choose who can start a conversation with you:

- **People in your communities** (default): anyone you share a community with.
- **Anyone on Ctrl Room**: people outside your communities can find you by your exact username.
- **Nobody**: no new conversations. The ones you've already accepted keep working.

## Groups

Anyone in a group can rename it and add people (each person's setting still applies). Use **Leave group** in the conversation's menu to leave. You keep what you could already read, and you won't get anything new. Someone in the group can add you back; you'll get it as a request.

## Mute, block and report

- **Mute** a conversation with the bell in its header: no sounds or alerts, and it stops counting toward the badge.
- **Block** someone from the conversation menu, the people list in a group, or a message's menu. They can't message you or start conversations with you, your 1:1 with them leaves your messages, and their messages in groups are hidden for you. They aren't told. Unblock people in **Settings → Privacy**.
- **Report** a message from its menu. Reports go to the people who run this Ctrl Room, with a copy of the message.

You can edit and delete your own messages. Deleted messages show as "Message removed".

## Notifications

New direct messages play the **dm** sound (Settings → Sounds, under Mentions, replies & DMs) and show a desktop alert if you turned those on. Do not disturb, muted conversations and ignored requests stay silent.
