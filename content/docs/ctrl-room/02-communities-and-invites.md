# Communities and invites

## Invite people

Mods, admins and the owner can create invite links in **Settings → Invite people**. Each link has:

- **Uses**: no limit, or 1, 5, 25 or 100 people.
- **Expires**: in a day, a week, 30 days, or never.

Copy the link and share it. Revoke a link with the trash button. A link stops working once it expires or runs out of uses.

## Public communities

Admins can make a community **public** in **Settings → Community**. Anyone signed in can then open `/c/your-link` and join without an invite. Private communities (the default) are invisible to anyone who isn't a member: their rooms, messages and members can't be seen or searched from outside.

## Change the name or icon

Admins and the owner can change the name, icon and description in **Settings → Community**.

## Leave a community

Open **Settings** and choose **Leave community**. You'll need a new invite to come back. The owner can't leave their own community.
