# Roles and moderation

Ctrl Room has four roles.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Chat, react, start threads, pin to the board, join calls, search. |
| **Mod** | Everything a member can, plus: post in announcement rooms, create and revoke invites, delete messages from members, remove or ban members, close any thread. |
| **Admin** | Everything a mod can, plus: add and delete rooms, edit the community's name, icon and description, make it public, make people mods or members. |
| **Owner** | Everything, plus making people admins. There is one owner per community. |

People can only act on others ranked **below** them. A mod can't remove another mod, and nobody can remove the owner.

## Change someone's role

Admins and the owner change roles in **Settings → People** with the role menu next to each person.

## Remove or ban

In **Settings → People**:

- **Remove** takes someone out of the community. They can come back with an invite.
- **Ban** removes them and stops them rejoining. Add a reason; only mods and up can see it. Banned people are listed under **Banned**, where you can **Unban** them.
