# Notifications and catch-up

## Quiet by default

Ctrl Room only pings you when someone **@mentions you** or **replies to a thread you started**. Rooms with new messages show in bold in the sidebar, and a number badge counts your mentions.

## Per-room settings

Use the bell menu in a room's header:

- **Mentions** (default): only @mentions and replies to you.
- **Everything**: a desktop alert for every new message in the room while the tab is in the background.
- **Muted**: no alerts; the room is dimmed in the sidebar.

## Desktop alerts

Choose **Turn on desktop alerts** at the bottom of the sidebar and allow notifications in your browser. Mention and reply alerts show when you're in another tab or another room. Click one to jump to the room.

## While you were gone

When you open a room with messages you haven't read, a recap appears at the top:

- How many new messages there are and who wrote them.
- The busiest threads, and their outcomes if they were closed.
- Decisions added to the room board.
- How many times you were mentioned or replied to.
- **Jump to first new message**.

Dismiss it with the ×. Opening a room marks it as read.
