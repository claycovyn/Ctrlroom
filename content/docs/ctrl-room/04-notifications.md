# Notifications and catch-up

## Quiet by default

Ctrl Room only pings you when someone **@mentions you** or **replies to a thread you started**. Rooms with new messages show in bold in the sidebar, and a number badge counts your mentions.

## Per-room settings

Use the bell menu in a room's header:

- **Mentions** (default): only @mentions and replies to you.
- **Everything**: a desktop alert for every new message in the room while the tab is in the background.
- **Muted**: no alerts; the room is dimmed in the sidebar.

## Notifications on your phone and computer

Turn them on in **Settings → Notifications** (or with the button at the bottom of the sidebar) and allow them when your device asks. You get one for an @mention, a reply to you, a direct message and a call starting in a room you use, even when Ctrl Room is closed. Tap one to open that message.

- **iPhone and iPad**: Apple only allows notifications for web apps installed from Safari (iOS 16.4 or later). In Safari choose **Add to Home Screen** (in the menu under the arrow-out-of-a-box icon), open Ctrl Room from its new icon and turn notifications on.
- In **Settings → Notifications** choose what you're notified about (mentions, replies, direct messages, calls) and set **quiet hours**. These follow your account to every device.
- Muted rooms and muted conversations never notify, and nothing from someone you blocked does.
- Notifications use your device's own sound. **Do not disturb** in Ctrl Room quiets the app's sounds only; on your phone, use quiet hours or Focus.

If the server hasn't set notifications up, the sidebar button turns on your browser's alerts instead, which show while the tab is open.

## Sounds

Ctrl Room plays short sounds once you've clicked or typed on the page:

- **Messages**: a soft tap for a new message in a room set to **Everything**, when you're in another room or tab.
- **Mentions, replies & DMs**: a chime when someone @mentions you or replies to you, unless the room is muted, and the dm sound for a new direct message, unless the conversation is muted.
- **Calls**: when you or someone else joins or leaves your call, and a ring when someone starts a call in the room you're looking at (until you join or dismiss it, 20 seconds at most).
- **Mic**: when you mute or unmute in a call.
- **Sent**: a tick when your message goes out. Off unless you turn it on.

Turn them off, set the volume, or switch each type on and off in **Settings → Sounds**, with a **Play** button to hear each one. Sound settings are saved on the device you set them on. **Do not disturb** turns every sound off. On iPhone the Ring/Silent switch also mutes them, and sounds start after your first tap.

## Your status

Members of a community see who's around. Each person shows as:

- **Online**: a filled dot.
- **Away**: a hollow ring, after 5 minutes without activity on the page (or with the tab in the background). Back to Online as soon as you're active.
- **In a call**: a headset (or camera, with video on), and which room the call is in.
- **Do not disturb**: a red dot with a bar.
- **Offline**: nothing.

Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar to choose **Online**, **Do not disturb** (no sounds) or **Appear offline** (nobody sees you; you still see everyone). The choice is saved on that device. The **People** panel groups members by status, and rooms with a call going list who's in it. You show as online in a community while you have it open. Only members of a community see each other's status.

## While you were gone

When you open a room with messages you haven't read, a recap appears at the top:

- How many new messages there are and who wrote them.
- The busiest threads, and their outcomes if they were closed.
- Decisions added to the room board.
- How many times you were mentioned or replied to.
- **Jump to first new message**.

Dismiss it with the ×. Opening a room marks it as read.
