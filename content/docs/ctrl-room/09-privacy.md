# Privacy and data

- **No ID checks** and no paid tier.
- **Who sees what:** a private community's rooms, messages, members and search results are only visible to its members. This is enforced by the database itself, not just the app.
- **Your profile** (display name and username) is visible to people who share a community or a direct message with you.
- **Direct messages** are only visible to the people in the conversation. You choose who can message you, and you can block people. See [Direct messages](/docs/ctrl-room/direct-messages).
- **Deleted messages** have their text removed; they show as "Message removed".
- **AI helpers:** when you mention @ctrl or @openctrl, your message and the recent messages in that room or thread are sent to the AI model configured for the server. See [AI helpers](/docs/ctrl-room/ai-helpers).
- **Calls** go through the LiveKit server configured for Ctrl Room and aren't recorded by Ctrl Room.
